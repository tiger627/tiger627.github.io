Tiger An is a BI & Data Consultant based in the Netherlands, specializing in Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and Dynamics 365 integrations. With over a decade of experience spanning SAP BI and the Microsoft data stack, he brings deep expertise in end-to-end data platform design — from ERP data modeling and medallion architecture to semantic layer development and operational reporting.

Tiger leads the Data & AI practice at a Dutch Microsoft partner consultancy, where he architects Fabric-based solutions for mid-market clients across finance, logistics, and distribution. His work spans the full delivery lifecycle: requirements analysis, solution design, data engineering (Lakehouse, Dataflows, Pipelines), semantic modeling (DAX, calculation groups), and self-service BI enablement.

Previously worked with SAP BW/BO across large-scale international BI programs, giving him a rare perspective on both SAP and Microsoft ecosystems — particularly valuable for clients navigating ERP migrations or hybrid landscapes.
